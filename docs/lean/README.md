# Lean proof

`BabyJubJubProof.lean` is a dependency-free Lean 4 refinement proof of the
arithmetic in `src/BabyJubJub.sol`. It proves:

- the affine formula is the standard twisted-Edwards addition formula;
- mixed extended-projective addition and doubling refine their modular-field
  specifications;
- double-and-add scalar multiplication refines those operations by induction;
- every ordinary addition and `2*z` in the Solidity implementation is below
  `2^256`, assuming the documented invariant that coordinates are below `Q`.

`BabyJubJubTateProof.lean` covers the reduced 8-Tate subgroup check. It proves:

- the binary `modExp` model equals ordinary modular exponentiation;
- the Solidity Miller-function arithmetic is transcribed exactly, including
  its inversion-free `N * D^7` construction;
- the fixed final exponent is `(Q - 1) / 8`;
- evaluation at the standardized full-order generator produces an eighth root
  of unity of exact order eight;
- all seven concrete nonidentity torsion representatives are on-curve and are
  rejected, including Miller-function zero/pole cases;
- the contract base point, proven to equal `[8]G`, is accepted — pinning the
  normalization of the closed-form Miller value, which the rejection witnesses
  alone cannot;
- in the cyclic group of order `8*R`, the kernel of this primitive character is
  exactly the subgroup generated by `[8]G`, of order `R`;
- `solidity_tate_accepts_iff_prime_order_subgroup` combines these facts with
  the standard reduced-Tate pairing representation laws and proves that the
  Solidity predicate accepts exactly prime-order-subgroup points;
- `solidity_tate_accepts_iff_canonical_order_check` proves the new predicate
  equivalent to the canonical `[R]P = 0` order check, stated abstractly in
  discrete-log coordinates (`canonicalOrderCheck`); the deployed
  `isInCorrectSubgroupAssumingOnCurve` double-and-add loop itself is not
  modeled.

Run it with:

```sh
lake build
```

The proof is a refinement proof of the implementation under its documented
preconditions. It deliberately does not claim that arbitrary, unreduced
`uint256` inputs are safe or that call sites satisfy those preconditions.

The development remains dependency-free, so it does not rebuild elliptic-curve
divisor theory or the general bilinearity/non-degeneracy theorem for the Tate
pairing. Those standard mathematical facts, together with the statement that
an affine point represents a particular discrete-log class, are exposed by
`TateEncodingLaws`; they are theorem parameters rather than hidden axioms. The
concrete Miller arithmetic, generator value, exact-order check, cyclic kernel,
identity exception, and final equivalence are checked by Lean.

`specAdd`, `specMixedAdd`, and `specDouble` transcribe the standard complete
twisted-Edwards affine law and the cited Hisil--Wong--Carter--Dawson extended
coordinate laws. Thus the checked result is implementation refinement to those
curve laws; this small dependency-free development does not rebuild the
general algebraic proof of those published formulas from field axioms.
